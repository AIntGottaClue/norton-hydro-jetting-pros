# Location research notes

Entity checklist per area: sewer jurisdiction, sewer vs septic, permits, housing, soils. Sources are linked on each page.

- Sewer jurisdiction: City of Norton water and sewer page (lateral is owner responsibility; main is the sewer authority's).
- Septic: Summit County Public Health sewage treatment systems and point of sale pages.
- Permits: City of Norton Zoning Office page (Summit County Building Standards handles building permits from May 26, 2026).
- Housing and history: City of Norton history pages, Nash Heights sewer records, Beacon Journal report on Brookside Greens.
- Soils: Summit County soil report (moderately well drained, clay-enriched subsoil).
