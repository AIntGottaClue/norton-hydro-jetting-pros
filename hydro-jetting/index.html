How hydro jetting works

Hydro jetting cleans a drain from the inside using pressurized water. For a Norton homeowner the practical questions are what the water does to the buildup, which lines suit it and what it does not fix.

What happens inside the line

A flexible hose carries water to a nozzle that is fed into the drain line through an access point such as a cleanout. The nozzle sends jets of water in several directions, and the thrust pulls the hose forward through the pipe. The jets loosen grease, sludge, scale and roots from the wall, and the flow carries the debris downstream.

The key difference from a cable is coverage. A cable cuts a path through the middle of a blockage and leaves the wall coated. Jetting works around the full circumference, which is why it is used for buildup and recurring clogs. The nozzle type and the water pressure are chosen to fit the pipe and what is in it.

What it suits and what it does not

Jetting suits intact pipe with buildup, including grease, sludge, mineral scale and root growth at joints. It does not repair broken pipe, close a gap or straighten an offset, and it should not be used on pipe that a camera shows is too fragile. That is why looking inside first is part of good practice.

In Norton the line from the house to the main belongs to the property owner, and the main belongs to the sewer authority that serves the address. Jetting is a tool for the owner's side of that line. Septic systems are a separate topic, since jetting cleans pipe but cannot change how a drain field absorbs water.

Worth knowing

01

Access matters

A cleanout or other access point lets the hose enter the line. Without one, a fixture trap or vent may need to be used, which is more limited.

02

Pipe condition decides

Pipe material, wall condition and any offsets all affect whether jetting is a good fit.

03

Cleaning is not repair

If a camera finds damage, cleaning may help for a while but the damage still needs attention.

Questions about how hydro jetting works

Does hydro jetting damage pipes?

On sound pipe with the right nozzle and pressure, jetting is a standard cleaning method. Fragile or badly corroded pipe may not be a good candidate, which is why the condition of the line is checked first.

What is the difference between jetting and a drain cable?

A cable cuts through the center of a blockage. Jetting cleans the full wall of the pipe with moving water, so it removes buildup a cable leaves behind.

Can jetting clear tree roots?

It can cut and flush roots that have grown into a line, but it does not seal the joint they entered through, so roots can return.
